About the company

A focused owner of residential property.

Statisfie LLC is the holding entity for our residential acquisitions. It takes title to the homes we buy, carries the obligations that come with ownership, and is the party on every purchase agreement we sign.

  • Principal, not agent.We buy for our own account. We do not represent buyers or sellers as a broker or agent.
  • Everything in writing.Price, payments and responsibilities are set out in a signed agreement before anything changes hands.
  • Obligations kept current.Payments, property taxes and insurance on the homes we hold are paid on time and documented.

How we work

Acquire. Hold. Steward.

A simple model, run carefully. Every home we take on is one we intend to keep in good standing.

01

Acquire

We purchase single-family homes directly from owners and investors, including on seller-financed terms when that serves both sides.

02

Hold

Statisfie LLC holds title and carries the property: payments, taxes and insurance are kept current and on record.

03

Steward

We keep each home occupied and cared for, with owner-occupants who intend to make it their own.
